Kerno – Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

Kerno is a word game for Android. This policy explains what information the game uses and why. In short: Kerno itself does not collect personal information. If you choose to use Google Play Games, your scores are shared with Google to show leaderboards.

Information stored on your device

Kerno stores your best score, your sound and vibration settings and whether you have seen the how-to-play screens on your device. This data never leaves your device, except as part of your own Android device backup if you have backups turned on. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Google Play Games (optional)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games, Kerno sends the score of each finished game to Google Play Games Services so it can appear on the daily, weekly and all-time leaderboards. The Google Play Games software in the app also sends Google diagnostic and usage data to keep the service stable. Google processes your Play Games profile (such as your player name and avatar) and your scores under the Google Privacy Policy. Other players can see your player name and score on the leaderboards. You can play Kerno without signing in; your scores then stay on your device.

What Kerno does not do

Kerno does not show ads, does not add its own analytics or tracking, does not ask for your name or email, and does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files. We do not sell or share any information with third parties.

Children

Kerno is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
